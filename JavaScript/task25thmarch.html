About NSS Blood Donation Camp

NSS Blood Donation Camp is an initiative by the National Service Scheme (NSS) to encourage people to donate blood and save lives. Blood donation is a noble act that can help patients in need of blood transfusion due to accidents, surgeries, or medical conditions. By donating blood, you can make a difference in someone's life and contribute to the well-being of society.
